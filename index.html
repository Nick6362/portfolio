About

Statistics in service of better health decisions.

My work sits at the intersection of biostatistics, data science, and public health. I’m especially interested in methods that make high-frequency, real-world health data more reliable and interpretable.

As a Graduate Research Assistant in Exercise Science, I develop reproducible R workflows that connect wearable sensor data with time-use diaries, improve data quality, and support behavioral research.

Wearable & time-use data
